# Privacy Policy — Augmented Adventures

**Last updated: June 10, 2026**

Augmented Adventures ("the app", "we", "us") is developed and operated by Janson Quinlan Prieb. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and what rights you have.

---

## 1. What we collect

### Information you provide
- **Email address** — if you create an account or sign in with email. Used only for authentication.
- **Display name** — optional username you set in your profile. Shown on the leaderboard if you opt in.
- **Avatar selection** — a visual avatar you choose inside the app.

### Information generated automatically
- **Gameplay progress** — which artworks you have scanned, which hunts you have completed, scores and times. Stored so your progress is restored if you reinstall or get a new device.
- **App usage events** — anonymised events such as "hunt started" or "artwork scanned." Used to understand how the app is used and fix problems. These events do not include your name, email, or any personally identifiable information unless you have signed into an account.

### Information the app accesses but does not store
- **Camera** — the camera is used only in real-time to recognise artwork via AR. No images or video are ever stored, uploaded, or transmitted.
- **Photos** — only accessed if you tap "Save" to save an AR snapshot to your own photo library. We never access your existing photos.

---

## 2. What we do NOT collect
- Location data
- Microphone audio
- Contacts
- Health or fitness data
- Financial information (purchases are handled entirely by Apple)
- Any data from children — the app is rated 4+ and does not contain features directed at children under 13

---

## 3. How your data is used

| Data | Why |
|------|-----|
| Email + user ID | Log you into your account and restore your progress |
| Display name + avatar | Show on leaderboard (only if you participate) |
| Gameplay progress | Resume where you left off; show your trophies and stats |
| App usage events | Understand which features are used; identify bugs |

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

---

## 4. Third-party services

The app uses the following services. Each has its own privacy policy.

| Service | Purpose | Privacy policy |
|---------|---------|----------------|
| **Supabase** | Account authentication and cloud progress storage | https://supabase.com/privacy |
| **PostHog** | Anonymous product analytics | https://posthog.com/privacy |
| **RevenueCat** | In-app purchase management (future paid hunts) | https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy |
| **Apple Sign In** | Optional sign-in method | https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy |

Supabase servers are located in the United States. By creating an account you consent to your account data (email, display name, gameplay progress) being stored there.

---

## 5. Your rights

- **Access** — you can view the data stored in your profile at any time from the app.
- **Correction** — you can update your display name and avatar in the app settings.
- **Deletion** — you can permanently delete your account and all associated data from the app: open the menu (top-left) → Delete Account. This action is immediate and cannot be undone.
- **Data portability** — to request a copy of your data, contact us at the address below.

---

## 6. Data retention

- Account data is retained until you delete your account.
- Anonymous analytics events are retained by PostHog per their data retention settings (default 7 years, anonymised).
- If you use guest mode (no account), no personal data is stored outside your device.

---

## 7. Security

Authentication tokens are stored in your device's encrypted Keychain, not in plain storage. All network communication uses HTTPS. We do not have access to your Apple Sign In credentials.

---

## 8. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
